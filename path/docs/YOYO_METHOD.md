# Yo-Yo Intermittent Recovery Test Level 1

SOCCERMRGAMIFICATION stores the total distance covered in the Yo-Yo Intermittent Recovery Test Level 1 (YYIR1), the test date, an optional final level and notes.

## Interpretation used by the MVP

The UI shows a **descriptive reference band**, not a medical threshold and not a universal soccer standard. The bands used in the MVP are based on a recent SOCCERIndex classification that reports the following YYIR1 ranges: <900 m, 900–1,100 m, 1,100–1,500 m, 1,500–2,000 m, 2,000–2,800 m and >=2,800 m for progressively higher recreational/amateur/semi-professional/professional reference groups.

These ranges must not be interpreted as saying that an individual player is or is not professional, nor as an eligibility, health or injury-risk threshold. Age, sex, playing level, position, protocol execution and testing conditions affect the result.

The app therefore recommends repeating the test under comparable conditions and following the individual change over time. YYIR1 has demonstrated reliability and the ability to discriminate between groups in soccer samples, but the appropriate reference population matters.

The application intentionally does not convert a YYIR1 result into a VO2max estimate or an injury-risk score.
